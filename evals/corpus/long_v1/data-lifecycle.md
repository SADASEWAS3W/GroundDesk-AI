# Team Access Export and Account Deletion Handbook

## Distinguish membership from personal account deletion

Managing team access, exporting data, and deleting an account are different operations. Removing a team member changes their access to that team. Exporting produces a downloadable representation of selected data. Account deletion is a permanent operation with separate consequences. Before following instructions, identify which of these outcomes is intended rather than using the actions interchangeably.

Open Settings > Team to manage members. Invitations are sent by email, and each member is assigned a role. Viewer is read-only; Member can create and edit; Admin manages settings and members; Owner has full control including billing. Read the role description before deciding whether the request concerns permissions or removal of an account.

## Change access without confusing it with erasure

A member's role can be changed at any time. Removing a member revokes access immediately but preserves their contributions. This combination matters: the access change is immediate, but the documented membership action does not erase everything the person contributed. It should not be described using the permanent-removal rules for deleting a personal account.

For example, a team may need to remove access while retaining the work already contributed to shared projects. The member-removal description addresses that situation. If the request instead concerns deletion of the person's entire account, use the account-deletion instructions and their ownership requirements. Identifying the intended action prevents a support answer from mixing incompatible consequences.

## Choose the scope of an export

Open Settings > Data Management > Export to export data. Choose projects, tasks, comments, attachments, or everything. Export formats include CSV, JSON, and PDF. The scope selection answers what to export; the format selection answers how the export is represented. These are separate choices within the documented export workflow.

As a practical planning step, identify the information needed before choosing the scope. Someone preparing a task record may have a different objective from someone preparing to leave the platform. This is advice for using the available selections, not a promise about an import or restoration feature. An export should not be assumed to undo a later permanent deletion.

## Wait for background processing and retrieve the result

Large exports are processed in the background, and an email with a download link is sent when the export is ready. The notification marks readiness for download. It should not be confused with the password-reset email or with a monthly invoice message, which belong to other workflows and have different documented timing.

Exports are available for download for 7 days. This is the download availability window, not a promised processing time for every export. When discussing a missing download, distinguish an export that is still being prepared from a link whose availability period has elapsed. The source documentation provides the background-processing behavior and the availability window as separate facts.

## Prepare for permanent deletion

The account guide recommends exporting data before deletion. If you are the sole Owner of a team, transfer ownership before deleting your account. Treat the ownership requirement as a prerequisite rather than as a step to postpone until after the account has gone. The member-role descriptions explain why an Owner is different from a Viewer or Member, but do not replace the explicit transfer requirement.

To delete an account, open Settings > Account > Delete Account and confirm by typing the account email address. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All personal data, projects you own, and associated files are permanently removed within 30 days. The removal window is not an undo period; it describes the documented timing of permanent removal.

Review the action's scope before confirming. Removing a member preserves contributions, whereas deleting an account has the permanent consequences described above. The 7-day export download window and the 30-day data-removal window also belong to different operations. Keeping these distinctions explicit is important in a support response about leaving a team or closing an account.
